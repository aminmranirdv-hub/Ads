# Monetization plan (owner said "both", 2026-11-21)

## Track A: paid clipping campaigns (money from week 1)
**What it is:** creators/brands post "campaigns" on clipping marketplaces and pay per 1,000 verified views of clips you post from THEIR content (their permission comes with it). Typical rate ~$1 / 1k views (range ~$0.30-$4), paid after the clip is approved and views verified; bot/bought views are filtered and get accounts banned.

**Where (owner signs up, it needs ID + payout details):**
- Whop Content Rewards marketplace: whop.com → Content Rewards / "Clipping" campaigns (largest, ~6,000 campaigns).
- Ssemble Clip Rewards (has a French page): ssemble.com/fr/clip-rewards
- cut.pro "championnats de clips" (a comedy one exists for stand-up clips).

**How we run it:**
1. Owner creates the account(s) and connects our TikTok/Instagram/Facebook pages.
2. Owner sends me the campaigns they joined (name + link to the source files + rules). French-language or comedy/entertainment campaigns first; English ones work too (we add FR subtitles).
3. I clip with the same pipeline (35-75 s, hook in 2 s, subtitles), follow each campaign's rules (required tag/caption/link), and post. Campaign clips get their own slot (1-2/day) on top of the 4 daily posts, or replace one when a campaign pays well.
4. Owner submits each posted link in the campaign dashboard (submission happens under their account), and gets paid.
- Never buy views, never repost the same clip on the same account, keep each campaign's disclosure (#ad / #partenariat) when asked.

## Track B: partnerships with comedians (legit page + paid show promotion)
**Offer:** free promotion to emerging francophone comedians (10k-300k followers): we clip their sets with FR/EN subtitles, credit + tag them + put their tickets link in the caption/bio. In exchange: written OK (a DM reply is enough) to post their clips, and later paid promotion of their tour dates.

**Targets (verify each official account yourself before DMing; I can't log into Instagram):** up-and-coming stand-ups from Jamel Comedy Club, Montreux Comedy, Marrakech du Rire "jeunes talents", Paname Art Café, Barbès Comedy Club, Fiesta des Suds, Moroccan/Algerian/Tunisian stand-up scenes (Casablanca, Rabat, Tunis comedy clubs), Belgian and Swiss scenes. Big names (Jamel, Gad, Kev) won't answer: aim for people who need visibility.

After 3-5 clips with good views, propose paid promo of a show date (price grows with reach; quote per post or per pack of 3).
