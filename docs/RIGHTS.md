# Rights & Reuse Policy

Stand-up specials, TV sets and club recordings are copyrighted, usually by
the comedian, a producer or a platform. A clip being viewable for free on
YouTube or TikTok does **not** grant permission to re-upload it. Every clip
needs a recorded basis for reuse before it's published.

## Status values (`rights.status`)

| status | meaning | publish? |
|---|---|---|
| `owned` | Footage the channel shot or generated itself | yes |
| `licensed` | Written licence covering short-form social reuse | yes |
| `written_permission` | Email or contract from the rights holder | yes |
| `platform_licensed` | Covered by an official clipping/affiliate programme's terms | yes, within those terms |
| `public_domain` | Verified public domain | yes |
| `creative_commons_ok` | CC licence that allows commercial use and derivatives (e.g. CC BY) | yes, with attribution |
| `pending` | Request sent, no answer yet | draft only |
| `fair_use_review` | Relying on commentary/fair dealing: needs a legal opinion first | draft only |
| `unknown` | Not checked | no |

`fair_use_review` exists because fair use (US) and quotation/parody
exceptions (France/EU, *droit de citation*) depend on the facts and on the
jurisdiction. Adding memes and captions doesn't automatically make a re-upload
fair use. Get advice before relying on it.

## Evidence to record (`rights.evidence`)

- Who granted it (name, role, organisation) and when
- What's covered (platforms, territories, duration, monetisation)
- Where the document is stored
- Required credit line

## Assets

| asset | source | status |
|---|---|---|
| SFX in `assets/sfx/` | Synthesised by `tools/generate_sfx.py` | owned |
| Stickers in `assets/stickers/` | Generated by `tools/generate_stickers.py` | owned |
| Fonts (Inter, DejaVu) | SIL OFL / free licences | ok for burned-in text |
| Music | none bundled; use licensed or platform-library tracks only | record per clip |
| Third-party memes/GIFs | not bundled | must be licensed |

## Permission request template

> Subject: Short-form clip licence request – <special/show>, <timestamp>
>
> Hello <name>,
>
> I run <channel>, a comedy shorts channel on TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. I'd like to publish a <duration>s vertical edit of <bit description> (<source>, <timestamp>), with subtitles in <languages>, full credit to <comedian> and a link to <official link>.
>
> We never alter the meaning of a joke, and our commentary is clearly separated from the performer's words. Could you let me know whether this is possible, and on what terms (platforms, monetisation, credit)?
>
> Thank you,
> <name>
