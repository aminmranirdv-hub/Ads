| Format | Proof it works | How we use it |
|---|---|---|
| **AI animal micro-dramas** (cheating, pregnancy, revenge, cliffhangers) | "Super Cat Dad" reportedly hit ~200M views in 6 months. In "The Cat Daddy Chronicles," single episodes reportedly reach hundreds of millions. | A daily Barnaby soap opera with a cliffhanger every episode, so people follow to see the next one. |
| **Cheating-fruit / object love stories** | The strawberry-eggplant video passed 26M views. Fruit Love Island reportedly gained 3M+ followers in 9 days. | Serialized dating drama: "Barnaby's Love Island." |
| **Cats doing human jobs** (fast food, news anchor, chef) | Spreading across Reels, TikTok and Shorts with millions of views. AI "working cat" accounts on Xiaohongshu grew fast. | Barnaby as boss, CEO, cashier, news anchor, judge. |

| **"Man-cat" reactions** (drinking, swearing, sighing like a tired dad) | Your reference reel: 931K likes. | Barnaby's after-work rituals: the shot, the sigh, the "kurwa." |
| **Music cats** (clapping, singing, dancing) | "Clapping Cat" reportedly ~220M views; "Knight Cat" ~210M. | One music post a week, synced to a trending sound. |

**Why these win:** short (7–15s), a shock in the first second, a recurring character people recognize, and an open loop ("Part 2?") that drives follows.

## 2. Honest expectations
- **500K followers in 30 days is possible but rare.** It happens when one series explodes, like Fruit Love Island. For a new account, a realistic good month is **20K–100K**. If one series goes viral, 500K+ can happen.
- What we control is volume, consistency, hooks and series. Plan for that and give the algorithm many chances to pick a winner.
- **Watch-outs:**
  - Label posts with Instagram's "AI info" tag. Hidden AI can get posts down-ranked.
  - Alcohol and swearing can limit reach and ads, so keep those to about 1 in 7 posts.
  - Never reuse other creators' characters, clips or sounds.

## 4. Posting rhythm
- **2 posts a day** on Instagram Reels, with the same clip on TikTok and YouTube Shorts (free extra reach).
- **Best times (test):** 12:00–13:00 and 19:00–21:00 in your audience's timezone.
- **Every post:** a hook in the first second, burned-in captions, 7–15s, and an ending that says "Part 2?" or asks a question.
- **Reply to the first 30 comments** within an hour. Pin the funniest comment.
