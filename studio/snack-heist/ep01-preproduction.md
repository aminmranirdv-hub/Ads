# "The Snack Squad," Episode 1: "The Cookie Mission"
Original IP · Kids 3–7 · 16:9 1920×1080 · 24 fps · target 6:45 (max 8:00)
**Lesson:** *It's better to ask than to sneak, and sharing makes everyone happy.*

---

## PHASE 1: Concept (chosen)
At night, three pet friends go on a secret "mission" to get the last honey-oat cookie from the top of the kitchen counter. Everything goes wrong in funny ways. When they finally reach it, the lazy cat swallows it whole. Then the lights come on: their girl, Lily, laughs and says, "You could have just asked!" She shares pet-safe treats with everyone.

---

## PHASE 3: Character sheets (locked designs, reuse for every episode)

### NIBS: the planner (hamster)
- **Look:** a round golden-cream hamster with a white belly and cheeks, pink round ears, a tiny pink nose and big glossy black eyes with two highlights. Wears a **blue eye mask** ("mission mask") and a **tiny yellow tool belt** with a crayon.
- **Size:** 1 unit tall (the smallest). Body plus head is one round shape ("bean"), with short arms and big feet.
- **Colors:** #F2C27B fur, #FFF5E6 belly, #3D8BFF mask, #FFD23F belt.
- **Personality:** a clever, bossy-but-sweet leader who loves plans. **Weakness:** impatient, never asks for help. **Strength:** brave and creative.
- **Voice:** quick and bright, a little kid-hero (Kokoro `am_puck`, pitch +350 cents, speed 1.1).
- **Signature gesture:** pushes the mask up with one paw, then points forward: *"Mission time!"*
- **Expressions:** determined squint, cheeks puffing when frustrated, big shiny eyes when hopeful.
- **Typical reaction:** freezes like a statue when caught.

### BUBBLES: the joker (goldfish)
- **Look:** a bright orange goldfish with flowing white-tipped fins and huge round eyes, living in a **round glass bowl on a red toy skateboard**. Tiny bubbles always rise from the bowl.
- **Size:** the bowl is 1.4 units tall. Moves by rocking the bowl so the skateboard rolls.
- **Colors:** #FF8A1F body, #FFFFFF fin tips, #E8262E skateboard, clear blue water.
- **Personality:** cheerful, silly, optimistic. **Weakness:** forgets things every few seconds (a running gag). **Strength:** never gives up and makes everyone laugh.
- **Voice:** bubbly and giggly (`af_sky`, pitch +300 cents, speed 1.05). Says *"Blub-tastic!"*
- **Signature gesture:** a happy spin inside the bowl, which makes a little splash.
- **Typical reaction:** *"Wait… what are we doing?"*

### MOCHI: the sleepy giant (cat)
- **Look:** a big fluffy **grey cat** with a white chest and paws, round chubby body, half-closed sleepy eyes (green), a pink nose, a long fluffy tail, and a tiny **red bell collar**.
- **Size:** 3 units tall when sitting (the biggest), so she's the base of the tower.
- **Colors:** #9AA3AD fur, #FFFFFF chest, #7FD08A eyes, #E8262E collar.
- **Personality:** lazy, sweet, few words, always hungry. **Weakness:** falls asleep anywhere and eats anything. **Strength:** tall and strong, gives the best hugs.
- **Voice:** slow, low and cozy (`am_echo`, pitch −50 cents, speed 0.85). Says *"Mmm… snack?"*
- **Signature gesture:** a huge slow yawn and stretch.
- **Typical reaction:** opens one eye and says *"…Hm?"*

### LILY: their girl (6-year-old)
- **Seen as:** mostly **pink bunny slippers, pajama legs and hands** (a low pet's-eye camera, which is also cheaper to animate). Full body appears once at the end, sitting on the floor with them.
- **Voice:** warm and kind (`af_heart`, pitch +200 cents).

## PHASE 2: Script (6:45)

**SCENE 1: The Hook (0:00–0:20) · Living room, night, moonlight**
EXTREME CLOSE-UP: Nibs' eyes behind the blue mask. Silence. A clock ticks.
**NIBS** *(whisper)*: Tonight… we get… the cookie.
*(Dramatic sting. Smash cut to the title card: "THE SNACK SQUAD · The Cookie Mission")*

**SCENE 2: The Team (0:20–1:20)**
Nibs slides down a curtain like a spy. Bubbles rolls in on the skateboard and bumps the sofa: *boing*.
**BUBBLES:** Blub-tastic! I'm here! Um… why am I here?
**NIBS:** The mission, Bubbles!
**BUBBLES:** Ooooh, the mission! …What mission?
Nibs points at Mochi, asleep on a cushion and snoring.
**NIBS:** Mochi! Wake up! Mission time!
**MOCHI** *(one eye opens)*: …Hm? *(closes it again)*
**NIBS:** There's a cookie.
**MOCHI** *(both eyes pop open)*: …Snack?

**SCENE 3: The Plan (1:20–2:10)**
Nibs unrolls a crayon drawing on the floor: the kitchen, a jar at the top of the counter, stick-figure versions of the three friends.
**NIBS:** The last honey-oat cookie is in the jar, way up on the counter. Step one: cross the slippery floor. Step two: sneak past the moonbeams. Step three: climb to the top!
**BUBBLES:** Can I be the climber?
**NIBS:** You're a fish.
**BUBBLES:** A *climbing* fish!
**MOCHI:** Can I be the… sleeper?
**NIBS:** Everybody ready? Mission time!

**SCENE 4: The Slippery Floor (2:10–3:00) · Kitchen doorway**
Shiny tiles. Nibs tiptoes, slips and spins like a top. Bubbles' skateboard zooms across and loops around a chair leg ("Wheeeee!"). Mochi just walks across normally and yawns. Nibs, dizzy: *"Step one… complete."*

**SCENE 5: The Moonbeams (3:00–3:45)**
Stripes of moonlight cross the floor through the window blinds.
**NIBS:** Don't touch the moonbeams. They're… super-secret lasers!
Nibs does a tiny somersault between the beams. Bubbles limbo-rolls under one. Mochi lies down *in* a moonbeam because it's warm and cozy, and falls asleep. Nibs and Bubbles push her out together (a cute teamwork beat).

**SCENE 6: The Robot Vacuum (3:45–4:40)**
A *beep-boop*: the robot vacuum wakes up, its blue light blinking. It rolls toward them!
**BUBBLES:** It wants to eat us!
A chase in a circle around the kitchen island. Bubbles rolls, Nibs runs on top of Mochi's back like a rodeo rider, and Mochi suddenly runs fast (her only fast moment). The vacuum bumps a wall, turns around and goes back to sleep: *beep… boop… zzz.* They all freeze, then sigh with relief.

**SCENE 7: The Tower (4:40–5:40) · At the foot of the counter**
**NIBS:** Step three! The tower!
Mochi sits at the bottom (*"Mmmf"*). Bubbles' bowl is carefully lifted onto Mochi's head (splash, *"Blub!"*). Nibs climbs up and stands on the bowl's rim. The tower wobbles left… right… With a big stretch, Nibs reaches the jar, lifts the lid (*shiiing*, a golden glow) and holds up THE COOKIE.
**NIBS** *(whisper)*: We… did it.

**SCENE 8: The Twist (5:40–6:05)**
Mochi sneezes. *"Ah… ah… ACHOO!"* The tower collapses in slow motion. The cookie flies up, spinning in the moonlight… and drops straight into Mochi's open mouth. *GULP.*
**Silence** (3 full seconds). A cricket chirps. Everyone stares.
**MOCHI:** …Oops.
**BUBBLES** *(tearful)*: The cookie…
**NIBS** *(sad, mask slipping down)*: I planned… everything.

**SCENE 9: The Lesson (6:05–6:35)**
*Click.* The lights turn on. Pink bunny slippers step into frame. Everyone freezes like statues.
**LILY:** What's all this noise? *(she crouches down and sees them)* Were you three hungry?
Nibs nods slowly, ashamed.
**LILY** *(laughs kindly)*: Silly friends! You could have just asked me!
She brings a little treat for each: sunflower seeds for Nibs, fish flakes for Bubbles, a fishy biscuit for Mochi.
**LILY:** Sharing is better than sneaking.
**NIBS** *(smiles, takes off the mask)*: Next time… we just ask!
**BUBBLES:** Ask what?
*(Everyone laughs.)*

**SCENE 10: The Button (6:35–6:45)**
Wide shot: Lily sits on the floor with all three, eating treats together, in warm light. Mochi is asleep again, and a tiny burp bubble floats up from her mouth: *pop*. Iris-out on Mochi's sleepy smile.
End card: **"Today's lesson: It's better to ask than to sneak!"** and **"See you next time, Snack Squad!"**

## PHASE 6–8 (summary)
- **Animation:** anticipation before every jump; squash on landings; Bubbles' water sloshes when the bowl moves; blinks every 3–5 seconds; lip sync driven by the voice track (per-frame mouth data); comic holds of 0.5–3 seconds.
- **Audio:** Kokoro voices (offline, Apache-2.0), original synthesized music (a sneaky pizzicato for the mission, a chase theme, a warm finale), Foley (tiles, splash, skateboard, vacuum), and real silence at the twist.
- **Edit:** MLT/Kdenlive assembly, cuts on action, one wipe after the plan, iris-out ending. Captions are optional (off by default for pre-readers, but a captions file is provided).
- **Export:** MP4, H.264, 1920×1080, 24 fps, AAC 192k, loudness about −14 LUFS (the YouTube standard).
