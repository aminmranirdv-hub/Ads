# "Sparkle Smile!": the M'Rani Family Brush Your Teeth Song (song #2)
Original lyrics. **Ages 1–5.** About 2:30 at a bouncy 108 BPM.
**Musical feel:** happy ukulele, glockenspiel and soft claps, with bubbly sound effects; kids' choir plus a cute little-boy lead (Hicham).
**Melody note:** newly written. Don't base it on any existing tooth-brushing song or nursery rhyme.

**[Intro: Hicham, spoken]** Beep-boop! Bedtime mission… sparkly teeth!
**[All]** Let's go!

**[Chorus: All]**
Brush-a, brush-a, round and round,
Tiny bubbles, foamy sound!
Top and bottom, front and back,
Sparkle smile, that's a fact!

**[Verse 1: Aziza]**
Just a tiny dot of paste, pea size,
Wet the brush and… wait, where's the… surprise?
*(spoken)* Oh! It's in my hand!

**[Verse 2: Amir]**
Front teeth first, it's kick-off time,
Brush the sides, the teeth all shine,
Count to ten and never stop,
*(spoken)* GOAL! The cleanest teeth on top!

**[Chorus: All]**

**[Verse 3: Abdel-Hamid]**
*(yawn)* Brush the top, now brush below,
Nice and gentle, nice and slow,
I built a robot, but you know,
*(spoken)* …I'll do it myself. Let's go!

**[Verse 4: Hicham]**
Brush my tongue, it tickles, hee!
Little circles, one, two, three,
Spit, spit, splash, and rinse it out,
*(spoken)* Look! Sparkle smile!

**[Bridge: counting to ten, everyone]**
Let's brush and count to ten:
One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten!
Sparkle smiles, shine again!

**[Final chorus: All, bigger]**
Brush-a, brush-a, round and round,
Tiny bubbles, foamy sound!
Top and bottom, front and back,
Sparkle smile, that's a fact!

**[Outro: spoken]**
**[Aziza]** Goodnight, Sparkle Team!
**[Hicham, to camera]** Now brush YOURS. I'll wait.
*(boing)*

## Healthy-habit notes (parents like accurate advice)
- A pea-size dot of toothpaste, gentle circles, top and bottom, front and back, brushing the tongue, then spitting and rinsing.
- The count to ten is a fun rhythm, not a real timer. The description recommends brushing for 2 minutes.
