## 5. Why this episode can travel (what's built into the video)
- **Hook in the first 5 seconds:** the cold open starts in the middle of the chaos ("NOBODY is hacking anything!" / "Too late."). That's the biggest single factor in retention.
- **Character intro cards** (0:05–0:26) so first-time viewers know everyone; new viewers come from Browse, not from episode 0.
- **A story beat every 20–40 seconds:** goal, memory freeze, red alert, slow-mo, crash, rebuild, the race against Mom's car.
- **A running gag** (Aziza forgetting) and a **button joke** ("False.") that viewers repeat and that make good Shorts.
- **A heart moment** (Aziza apologizes, the siblings rebuild together): the parent-approved value of teamwork and saying sorry.
