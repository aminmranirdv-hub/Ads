# M'RANI FAMILY: next episode ideas (vidIQ research, Nov 2026)

**Scan:** 6 vidIQ calls (30 of 150 free credits): 5 breakout-video searches (outliers, last 3–12 months, English) and 1 keyword-demand check.
**Channel:** no YouTube channel is connected to vidIQ yet, so this uses adjacent kids and family animation channels, not M'Rani's own data.
**Read these as research candidates, not view predictions.** "Breakout" means views relative to that channel's own average.

**Why this fits M'Rani:** small, often AI-made animation channels are breaking out with these patterns, so it's feasible at our size. Mechanism C lines up with our four characters almost one-to-one.

**Search demand:**
- "kids cartoons": about 780K searches per month, mostly Pakistan (49%), India (14%) and the US (5%).
- "cartoons for kids": about 637K per month.
- "family cartoon for kids": about 47K per month, down 61%. Generic "family cartoon" wording is fading, so titles should sell the **story**, not the genre.

## Episode concept cards (original stories; nothing copied)

### Ep. 2: "Someone Stole the Last Cookie… THEN We Found the Hidden Camera! 😱" ⭐ top pick
- **Mechanisms:** A + B
- **Story:** the cookie jar is empty, and a crumb trail leads everywhere. Aziza plays detective but forgets her clues. Amir "predicts" the culprit wrong, three times. Hicham hacks the old baby-monitor camera. The twist: Abdel-Hamid's snack-fetching robot did it, and it's still on the loose.
- **Hook (first 5 seconds):** an empty jar, a gasp, then "NOBODY leaves this house until we find the thief!"
- **Lesson:** don't accuse without proof, and shortcuts backfire. This merges the series bible's ideas #1 and #10.
- **Ratings:** audience fit ★★★★★ · originality ★★★★ · production feasibility ★★★★ (mostly one location, our existing sets) · evidence confidence: **medium-high**

### Ep. 3: "Strange Noises in Our House at Midnight… Who Was It?! 👀"
- **Mechanisms:** A + B
- **Story:** the siblings hear knocking at night while their parents are out. They set Home-Alone-style traps (Abdel-Hamid's inventions, Amir's football "defense line"). The "intruder" is Grandma, arriving early with a surprise, which is the heart moment.
- **Hook:** a dark hallway, a creaking sound, four flashlights clicking on.
- **Ratings:** fit ★★★★ · originality ★★★★ · feasibility ★★★ (night lighting, traps) · confidence: **medium**. Keep it funny, not scary, for ages 4–10.

### Ep. 4: "My 4-Year-Old Brother Used 1000 IQ to Outsmart the Whole Family 🤯"
- **Mechanism:** C (the 2.7M-view Short), stretched to long form
- **Story:** Hicham runs a chain of genius schemes to win "extra tablet time": fake chore charts, voice-assistant tricks, a smart-light "emergency". Each sibling falls for a different trick until they team up to out-plan him. The ending: he only wanted everyone to play together.
- **Hook:** Hicham's slow eyebrow raise, then "Phase one: complete."
- **Ratings:** fit ★★★★★ · originality ★★★★ · feasibility ★★★★ · confidence: **medium**

### Ep. 5: "If My Little Brother Was the MOM for a Day… 😱"
- **Mechanism:** C, as a role swap
- **Story:** Mom lets each kid be "the parent" for an hour. Aziza is strict and forgets everything. Abdel-Hamid's "parenting" is an app. Amir runs the house like football training. Hicham-as-mom is the funniest and the wisest.
- **Ratings:** fit ★★★★★ · originality ★★★ (role swaps are common, but our characters make it fresh) · feasibility ★★★★ · confidence: **medium**

### Ep. 6: "Someone Took Amir's Football… THEN the Chase Began! ⚽🚓"
- **Mechanisms:** A + B, an outdoor neighborhood chase
- **Story:** the siblings chase the football "thief" through the park. Amir's footwork, Hicham's map hacking and Abdel-Hamid's scooter invention all come into play. The "thief" is a shy new neighbor kid who just wanted to play, and the ending is a new friend.
- **Ratings:** fit ★★★★ · originality ★★★★ · feasibility ★★★ (new outdoor locations, so new pictures are needed) · confidence: **medium**

## Shorts series (mechanism C): post 2–3 per week, cut from the episodes
1. **"POV: you have 3 brothers"**: Aziza's daily chaos, 20–40 seconds.
2. **"1000 IQ Hicham"**: one genius toddler trick per Short.
3. **"Aziza forgot… AGAIN"**: a running gag with the loading-circle freeze.
4. **"If Amir was the coach of…"**: the family, school or Grandma's kitchen.
5. **"Abdel-Hamid's laziest inventions"**: one invention per Short.

## How to package (from what's breaking out)
- Write titles in the first person: "My brother…", "We found…", "Someone stole…".
- Build titles as **[event]… THEN [twist]!** with 1–2 emojis. Avoid generic "family cartoon" wording.
- Thumbnails: one huge expressive face plus the key object (empty cookie jar, flashlight, football), with 3 words or fewer of text.
- **Audience note:** kids'-cartoon search is heaviest in Pakistan and India, so Arabic, Urdu or Hindi and French dubs of top episodes could multiply reach cheaply. The voices are AI, so dubbing is easy.

## Limits of this scan
- It's a small sample. Many breakout examples come from English-learning story channels and Hindi cartoon channels: the mechanisms transfer, but those audiences aren't identical to ours.
- Breakout scores on tiny channels can be inflated.
- Once the M'Rani channel is live and connected to vidIQ, run **vidiq-next-video-planner** and **vidiq-channel-audit** for ideas based on the channel's own data.
