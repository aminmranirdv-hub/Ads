# AMIR: "The Fearless Wunderkind"
*Character bible v1. Use this for every episode, prompt and voice session.*

## Personality
- **Superpower:** reads people and predicts what happens next in a split second, on the football pitch and off it. *"He'll pass left. The ball will bounce twice. Mom will say no."* He's usually right.
- **Talents:** elite little playmaker at his youth football club (speed, reflexes, vision). He builds huge brick towns and draws detailed battle plans for everything, even breakfast.
- **Personality:** brave, high-energy, can't sit still, a natural leader.
- **Funny weaknesses:**
  - **Overconfident:** sure he's the smartest person in the room, adults included. He leaps before anyone can say "wait," which always causes chaos.
  - **Hates being treated like a little kid.** If anyone uses a baby voice, he crosses his arms and raises one eyebrow.
- **Heart:** fiercely protective of his friends. When someone he loves is in trouble, he's the first one running toward it.

## Voice and speaking style
- **Delivery:** quick, sharp and sure of himself, with grown-up words coming out of a tiny kid. That contrast is the comedy.
- **Speech habits:** questions adult logic ("Respectfully… that plan has three problems"), counts problems on his fingers, and gets offended at baby talk.
- **Catchphrases** (kid-friendly):
  - *"Don't talk to me like a kid. I already know how this ends."*
  - *"I predicted that."* (even when he obviously didn't)
  - *"Plan B! …I mean, that WAS the plan."*
  - *"Hand me the blueprint."*
- **Voice casting:** a bright, fast, confident little-boy cartoon voice, higher and quicker than Abdel-Hamid.

## Animation notes
- **Idle:** never still. He juggles the ball, bounces on his toes, spins.
- **Signature "prediction" moment:** time slows, his eyes narrow, and glowing arrows and dotted lines appear in the air showing where things will go (like a football tactics board). Then he moves before anyone else.
- **Signature gesture:** taps his temple, then points at you: "Called it."
- **Typical reactions:** an arms-crossed, eyebrow-raised glare at baby talk; a huge celebration slide on his knees when he's right; a sheepish "oops" grin when his jump goes wrong.

## Role in the series
**The action and the instinct of the team.** He is Abdel-Hamid's perfect opposite: **Abdel-Hamid thinks slowly and never moves; Amir moves instantly and thinks fast.** Together they're unstoppable, once they learn to listen to each other.

**Episode hooks built on him:**
- **"The Big Match":** his overconfidence almost loses the final, so he learns to pass to teammates.
- **"Brick City":** he builds a giant brick city, and Abdel-Hamid's lazy robot crashes into it.
- **"I Predicted That":** his predictions keep coming true, until the one time they don't.
