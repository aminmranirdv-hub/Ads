## Personality
- **Superpower:** the **digital-native genius**. He understands complex ideas instantly, fixes phones, finds anything online and figures out passwords before grown-ups find their glasses.
- **Cunning:** sharp, observant and resourceful. He knows every shortcut around grown-up rules and plays the "I'm just a baby" card when it suits him.
- **Maturity:** calm, quiet and alert. He gives grown-ups a look that says "I'm ten steps ahead of you." He usually is.
- **Funny weaknesses:**
  - **Screen-obsessed:** completely glued to his tablet. Taking it away is a national emergency.
  - **Too clever for his own good:** his shortcuts often cause bigger problems.
- **Heart:** he quietly watches everything, so he's the first to notice when a friend is sad. He shows love by fixing your stuff.

## Voice and speaking style
- **Delivery:** calm, slow and clear, with startlingly grown-up words from a tiny toddler voice. Then he switches to baby-cute on purpose when he's in trouble.
- **Speech habits:** quotes "a video I watched" as his source for everything. He speaks rarely but precisely: one sentence that solves the problem.
- **Catchphrases** (kid-friendly):
  - *"Give it back. I was in the middle of a tutorial."*
  - *"I watched a video about this."*
  - *"Have you tried turning it off and on again?"* (said to grown-ups, deadpan)
  - *"Who, me? I'm only four."* (fake-innocent smile)
- **Voice casting:** a soft, tiny toddler voice with a very calm, clever delivery.

## Animation notes
- **Idle:** sitting cross-legged, the screen glowing blue on his face, tapping very fast with tiny fingers.
- **Signature "hack" moment:** his eyes reflect scrolling code, tiny holographic icons (gears, lightbulbs, play buttons) float up from the tablet, and he looks up with a slow, knowing smile.
- **Signature gesture:** a single tiny finger-tap on the screen, then turning it around to show you: "Done."
- **Typical reactions:** a deep toddler gasp when the tablet is taken away; an "angel face" when caught; an unimpressed blank stare when grown-ups are slow.

## Role in the series
**The tech and the secret weapon of the team.** Grown-ups underestimate him, which is exactly why he wins.

**Built-in positive message for parents:** across the series, Hicham learns that **the best adventures happen when you look up from the screen**. He keeps his tech genius but discovers real-world play with his friends. This gives parents a reason to love the show, not just kids.

**Episode hooks built on him:**
- **"The Password":** the family Wi-Fi breaks, and only the 4-year-old can save movie night.
- **"Screen-Free Saturday":** no tablet for a whole day; can he still be a genius?
- **"The Video Said So":** he follows a tutorial a bit too literally, with hilarious results.
