# M'RANI FAMILY: Episode 2, "The Case of the Missing Cookie"
**YouTube title:** *Someone Stole the Last Cookie… THEN We Found the Hidden Camera! 😱 | M'Rani Family*
**Length:** about 4:30 · **Ages:** 4–10 + parents · **Lesson:** don't accuse without proof; shortcuts can backfire
**Mechanism (from vidIQ research):** a "who did it?" mystery, a chase, and a "THEN…" twist

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00–0:15): the hook
*Kitchen, morning. Close-up of a big glass cookie jar with ONE giant chocolate cookie inside, and a sticky note on it: "AZIZA'S – DO NOT TOUCH".*
**AZIZA** *(V.O., proud)*: My prize cookie. Saved all week.
*Smash cut: the same jar… EMPTY. Crumbs everywhere.*
**AZIZA** *(screaming)*: WHO. ATE. MY. COOKIE?!
*Music sting, then title card.*

## TITLE + MEET THE FAMILY (0:15–0:25)
Name cards, as in the songs (free).

## SCENE 1: The crime scene (0:25–1:15)
*Living room. Aziza paces in a detective hat, holding her purple planner as a notebook.*
**AZIZA:** Nobody leaves this house until we find the thief!
**AMIR** *(bouncing)*: I already know who did it. I predicted it yesterday.
**AZIZA:** Who?
**AMIR** *(points dramatically)*: HIM!
*Whip pan to ABDEL-HAMID on the beanbag, chewing.*
**ABDEL-HAMID** *(mouth full)*: Mmf… I'm eating CRACKERS. Different snack family.
**HICHAM** *(not looking up from tablet)*: Statistically, the person with crumbs on his shirt is guilty 87 percent of the time.
*Everyone stares at Abdel-Hamid's shirt. Crumbs. Gasp.*
**ABDEL-HAMID:** Those are cracker crumbs! I'm innocent!
**AZIZA** *(writing)*: Suspect number one… wait, what was his name again?
**ALL:** ABDEL-HAMID!

## SCENE 2: The investigation (1:15–2:30)
*Montage with music (Kling 4.0 action shots):*
1. **Aziza** examines the crumb trail on the floor with a magnifying glass.
2. **Amir** "re-enacts" the crime with his football: kick, dive, slide, crashing into a cushion.
3. **Abdel-Hamid** lazily rolls on his office chair to the jar and measures it with a ruler: "The thief had… small hands."
4. **Hicham** hacks the old baby monitor camera: "Accessing footage. Beep-boop."

**HICHAM:** Got it. The thief is on camera.
*Everyone crowds around the tablet.*
**AZIZA:** Play it!
*The tablet screen shows grainy night footage: a small shadow rolling toward the jar… with blinking lights.*
**AMIR:** It's a ghost!
**ABDEL-HAMID** *(slowly sitting up, eyes widening)*: …That's not a ghost.

## SCENE 3: THE TWIST (2:30–3:20)
*Abdel-Hamid's room. Under his bed: a little snack robot with a cookie-crumb mouth, "SNACK-O-BOT 3000" painted on its side.*
**ABDEL-HAMID** *(guilty)*: I built a robot to bring me snacks… so I'd never have to get up. I programmed it to find… "the best snack in the house."
**AZIZA** *(hands on hips)*: And it found MY cookie.
*Snack-o-bot burps a crumb. Beep.*
**AMIR:** So technically… I was right! It WAS him!
**HICHAM:** Technically, it was his robot. 50 percent right.

## SCENE 4: Making it right (3:20–4:05)
*Kitchen. The siblings bake new cookies together (Kling 4.0 action montage):*
- Abdel-Hamid stirs the bowl (he actually gets up!).
- Amir cracks an egg… perfectly, "Tactical precision!"
- Hicham sets the oven timer on his tablet.
- Aziza reads the recipe… "Step three… wait…" *(Hicham shows her the tablet)* "…thank you."

**ABDEL-HAMID:** I'm sorry, Aziza. I should have just asked instead of taking a shortcut.
**AZIZA** *(smiles)*: And I'm sorry, Abdel-Hamid. I blamed you without proof.
*Ding! A tray of perfect cookies.*

## BUTTON GAG + OUTRO (4:05–4:30)
*Four siblings, each with a cookie, smiling. Behind them, the Snack-o-bot quietly rolls toward the tray…*
**HICHAM** *(deadpan to camera)*: Case closed.
*Beep-beep! The robot grabs the whole tray and zooms away.*
**ALL:** SNACK-O-BOT!!!
*Chase out of frame, boing, then the end card: "Subscribe! Next episode: Strange Noises at Midnight 👀"*

## Production plan (what makes it look professional this time)
- **One shot per spoken line**, with only the **speaker talking**. Listeners get non-talking reaction shots.
- **Lip-sync on every spoken line**, so mouths match the words.
- **Action and montage shots in Kling 4.0** (the quality you liked), checked frame by frame.
- **A real music score** (mystery theme, chase, warm ending) and **real sound effects**, instead of code-made audio.
- **Scene transitions:** a magnifying-glass wipe between scenes, whip pans, and audio that runs a little ahead of the cut into the next shot.
- **New sets:** kitchen, Abdel-Hamid's bedroom, and the Snack-o-bot character, made consistent from the model sheets.
