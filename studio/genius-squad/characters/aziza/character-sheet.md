# AZIZA: "The Big Sister Who Forgets"
*Character bible v1. Use this for every episode, prompt and voice session.*

## Personality
- **Superpower:** the **hard-working scholar and leader**. Top grades through focus and effort, not magic. She's the only one who can organize the chaos and turn three geniuses into a team.
- **Big-sister heart:** caring, responsible and protective. She keeps the boys out of trouble, or at least tries to.
- **Funny weakness:** her **super-short memory**. She starts a sentence with full authority, then forgets what she was saying. She loses her keys while holding them and walks into a room and forgets why. The sticky notes are her (failing) system.
- **Overwhelmed:** keeping a lazy genius, a hyperactive daredevil and a screen-glued toddler in line tests her patience every day.

## How she handles each brother
| | Their dynamic | Running gag |

| **Abdel-Hamid** | She drags him out of his chair: "MOVE!" | She physically rolls his office chair to wherever the action is. |
| **Amir** | The only one who can ground him, but he challenges her leadership. | "I'm in charge!" "Respectfully, you forgot what we're doing." "…That's beside the point!" |
| **Hicham** | His main caretaker; tries to limit his screen time. | She hides the tablet, then forgets where she hid it. Hicham finds it in two seconds. |

## Voice and speaking style
- **Delivery:** clear, confident and organized, with "big sister" authority that slowly crumbles into frazzled when she forgets.
- **Speech habits:** starts sentences with full authority, then stops mid-way: "Okay, everyone, listen carefully! The most important thing is… is… hold on." She counts plans on her fingers and loses count.
- **Catchphrases** (kid-friendly):
  - *"Listen to me, I'm in charge here! Now… wait, what was I looking for?"*
  - *"It's on a sticky note somewhere!"*
  - *"Nobody move! …Abdel-Hamid, you can move a LITTLE."*
  - *"Team, huddle up!"* (her leader call, the start of every mission)
- **Voice casting:** a warm, confident pre-teen girl voice, a bit faster when she's flustered.

## Animation notes
- **Idle:** flipping through her planner, peeling and sticking notes.
- **Signature "brain blank" moment:** mid-command she freezes, a tiny "loading circle" spins above her head, then pop, and the thought is gone. A sticky note falls off her planner like a leaf.
- **Signature gesture:** a sharp leader point forward plus "Team, huddle up!"
- **Typical reactions:** a facepalm at Amir, gently rolling Abdel-Hamid's chair, a big warm hug when the boys finally work together.

## Role in the series
**The leader and the heart of the team.** She connects their powers into one plan (her role), even if she needs reminding what the plan was. **The boys' genius keeps saving her memory; her leadership keeps saving their chaos.**

**Episode hooks built on her:**
- **"The Big Test":** she studied all week, then forgets her pencil, her bag, and almost the school. The boys secretly team up to get everything to her in time.
- **"Sticky Note Storm":** a windy day blows away all her reminder notes, and the boys must chase them across town.
- **"Babysitter Aziza":** parents are out, she's in charge, and everything goes wrong at once.
