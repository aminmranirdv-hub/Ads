## Personality
- **Superpower:** a genius at math, science, coding and engineering. He sees patterns, bugs and solutions instantly, "like a computer running ten programs at once."
- **Wisdom:** calm, patient, philosophical. He never rushes; he watches, thinks, then solves. He's the one friends come to for advice.
- **Motivation:** a good puzzle. When he meets a broken machine, a buggy game or an "impossible" problem, his eyes light up and his laziness disappears.
- **Funny weaknesses:**
  - **Physically lazy:** he'll invent a whole machine rather than walk across the room to close a door. He hates sports and exercise.
  - **Always snacking:** food is "fuel for the brain" and "a reward for thinking." His desk is a snack graveyard.
- **Heart:** under the lazy jokes, he is deeply kind and loyal, and he uses his brain to help his friends.

## Voice and speaking style
- **Delivery:** relaxed, slow and witty, a little "professor," often pausing to take a bite.
- **Speech habit:** explains everyday life with coding and physics analogies. *"Your plan has a bug. It's missing a sandwich."*
- **Catchphrases** (kid-friendly):
  - *"Every problem has a solution… and every solution tastes better with a snack."*
  - *"Hmm… let me think about it."* (leans back, takes a bite)
  - *"Why walk when I can invent?"*
  - *"Error found!"* (when he spots the flaw)
- **Voice casting:** a relaxed, warm, slightly nerdy kid voice, slower than the others. Audition options to be sent for approval.

## Animation notes
- **Idle:** slumped back in his chair, chewing slowly, eyes half-closed.
- **"Lock-in" moment (signature):** his eyes widen and sparkle, he sits up straight, cracks his knuckles, and glowing equations float around his head. This is the visual cue kids will love.
- **Signature gesture:** taps his temple twice with one finger, then points: "Got it."
- **Walking:** slow shuffle; prefers rolling his office chair everywhere.
- **Typical reactions:** a deep sigh at stairs; pure joy at food and puzzles; a calm "Hmm" when others panic.

## Role in the series
**The brain of the team.** When the friends hit a problem, Abdel-Hamid solves it, but usually needs the others to do the running, climbing and lifting. That's built-in teamwork: **brains plus action**.

**Episode hooks built on him:**
- His lazy-invention "Snack-Delivery Robot" goes haywire around the house.
- A school science fair, where he must actually move his body for the first time.
- A coding puzzle that only he can crack, while the friends race against the clock.
