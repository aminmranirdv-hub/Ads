# Barnaby Purrkins Universe — Character Bible & Episode Pitches

## 1. Star character: BARNABY PURRKINS

**Reusable character reference (paste into every AI image/video prompt):**

> Barnaby, a chunky adult male cat, cream fur with soft ginger ticking, a dark ginger stripe across the forehead shaped like one thick, permanently judgmental unibrow, a round flat face with full cheeks, mismatched eyes (left eye amber-gold, right eye mint-green), short stubby legs, a round barrel belly, a thick fluffy tail with a ginger tip, a small notch in the top of his left ear, tiny gold-rimmed half-moon reading glasses on the tip of his nose, and a worn tan leather fanny pack strapped across his chest. Expressive, human-like facial expressions. Same character in every shot.

### Recurring cast
- **Doug**: a golden retriever, Barnaby's unpaid "intern." Overly enthusiastic, a little dim, loves everything. Wears a lanyard that says "VISITOR."
- **Pamela**: a city pigeon and gossip reporter with a tiny microphone. Talks fast, always has "sources."
- **Gerald**: a goldfish, Barnaby's attorney. Never speaks; holds up tiny legal signs.
- **The Landlord**: the human owner. We only ever see hands, feet, or the back of their head. Barnaby calls them "the Landlord."

### Universe rules
1. Barnaby must stay dignified until the punchline breaks him.
2. Every episode ends with the Slow Glasses Push and one catchphrase, or a twist on one.
3. Visual style: **cinematic photoreal**. Shallow depth of field, warm apartment lighting, and real-world props at cat scale.
4. Audio: original voice lines only, plus royalty-free or original music and SFX.

## 2. Ten original scenarios (ranked by entertainment potential)

### #1 — "The Performance Review"
- **Description:** Barnaby sits at a tiny desk and gives the Landlord their annual performance review, with a clipboard and printed charts showing "Treat Delivery Speed" and "Lap Availability."
- **Main joke:** The review is brutal, until he reaches the section "Ear Scratches" and loses all composure while purring.
- **Viral hook (0–2s):** Close-up of Barnaby sliding a paper across the desk: *"Sit down. This won't take long."*
- **Originality angle:** It flips the pet/owner power dynamic using a workplace ritual everyone dreads. It's relatable to every office worker.

### #2 — "Doug's First Day"
- **Description:** Barnaby onboards Doug the golden retriever as his new intern. HR orientation, safety video, ID badge.
- **Main joke:** Doug fails every task (he eats the stapler, fetches the fire alarm). Then a tennis ball rolls by and Barnaby is the one who chases it.
- **Viral hook:** Doug crashes through the door, face-first, wearing a lanyard: *"I'M HERE FOR THE JOB!"*
- **Originality angle:** An odd-couple workplace sitcom set up as the franchise pilot. It establishes the duo.

### #3 — "Breaking News: The Cucumber Incident"
- **Description:** Pamela the pigeon live-reports from the kitchen windowsill about "an unidentified green object" found near Barnaby's food bowl. Barnaby gives a statement at a press podium.
- **Main joke:** Barnaby stays perfectly calm through the whole press conference. Then the Landlord's hand places a second cucumber on the podium and he launches straight off-screen.
- **Viral hook:** A red "BREAKING NEWS" banner and a pigeon screaming *"WE ARE LIVE!"*
- **Originality angle:** A news-parody format with an animal anchor. It plays on a familiar cat fear without reproducing any existing clip.

### #4 — "Couples Therapy (With the Laser Dot)"
- **Description:** Barnaby goes to couples therapy with his "ex," the red laser dot. The therapist is a calm owl.
- **Main joke:** *"It always leaves when I get close."* The dot shows up on the couch, and therapy breaks down into chaos.
- **Viral hook:** Barnaby on a therapy couch, tissue in paw: *"I gave it everything."*
- **Originality angle:** A relationship-drama parody where the partner is an inanimate object. Highly shareable "that's my ex" humor.

### #5 — "The 3 A.M. Ballad"
- **Description:** A dramatic, original power ballad Barnaby sings at 3 a.m. outside the bedroom door, titled "Open the Door (I Have Nothing to Say)."
- **Main joke:** He belts out a soaring song about needing to come in. The door opens, he looks inside for one second, then walks away. Last line: *"…Noted."*
- **Viral hook:** Pitch-black hallway, spotlight snaps on, Barnaby hits a huge note.
- **Originality angle:** Original lyrics and an original melody concept. Every cat owner has lived this, so it's very commentable.

### #6 — "Barnaby, CEO: Hostile Takeover"
- **Description:** Barnaby holds a boardroom meeting to announce he's acquiring the neighbor's balcony. Gerald the goldfish holds up legal signs.
- **Main joke:** The deal is about to close, until the neighbor's tiny Chihuahua walks in and Barnaby signs over his entire empire out of fear.
- **Viral hook:** Barnaby slams a paw on a glass boardroom table: *"Gentlemen. We're going vertical."*
- **Originality angle:** Corporate-thriller parody with a silent fish lawyer. Strong recurring-gag potential.

### #7 — "Caught: The Midnight Fridge Heist"
- **Description:** A heist-movie setup. Barnaby and Doug plan a fridge raid with blueprints and laser grids made of sticky notes.
- **Main joke:** Everything goes perfectly, until the fridge light comes on and reveals the Landlord already sitting in the dark, eating the target ham.
- **Viral hook:** Night-vision POV: *"Phase one: the ham."*
- **Originality angle:** A heist-genre parody with a "caught in the act" twist that hits the owner, not the cat.

### #8 — "Celebrity Meltdown: The Vet Red Carpet"
- **Description:** Barnaby arrives at the vet like a celebrity on a red carpet, with paparazzi flashes (Pamela and pigeon friends) and sunglasses.
- **Main joke:** He poses like a diva, until the receptionist says the word "thermometer." Smash cut: he's trying to escape through the air vent in full gown.
- **Viral hook:** Slow-motion sunglasses removal under camera flashes.
- **Originality angle:** Celebrity-glam parody colliding with a mundane terror. Great looping visual.

### #9 — "Job Interview at the Fish Market"
- **Description:** Barnaby applies for a job as a fishmonger. The interviewer is a stern seagull.
- **Main joke:** Q: *"Where do you see yourself in five years?"* Barnaby, eyes locked on the fish: *"…Inside that tuna."*
- **Viral hook:** Barnaby in a tiny tie, sweating: *"I'm very passionate about fish. Professionally."*
- **Originality angle:** The human-job format with an obvious, escalating conflict of interest.

### #10 — "The Unexpected Roommate"
- **Description:** A mouse moves in, and instead of chasing it, Barnaby becomes its overprotective roommate: chore charts, a shared Netflix password.
- **Main joke:** The mouse throws a loud party. Barnaby bangs on the wall with a broom: *"Some of us have a nap at 2, 4, 5, 7 and 9!"*
- **Viral hook:** A tiny mouse with a suitcase knocks on a mouse hole that now has a "FOR RENT" sign.
- **Originality angle:** An unlikely-friendship and bad-roommate sitcom. A wholesome-plus-chaos mix that does well for shares.
