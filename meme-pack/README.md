# Original Meme Pack (royalty-free)

Meme-style overlays and sound effects for Shorts. **Everything here was made from scratch**, so you can use it in monetized videos with no copyright claims.

Regenerate or extend it with: `python3 build_pack.py`

| Folder | What's inside | How to use |
|---|---|---|
| `stickers/` | 46 transparent PNGs: reaction text (BRUH, PLOT TWIST, CAUGHT IN 4K, THE AUDACITY, NOPE, OH NO.), story cards (3 HOURS LATER, Meanwhile…, ONE ETERNITY LATER, POV:), challenge tags (ROUND 1, WINNER, FAIL, NAILED IT), verdict stamps (GUILTY, CASE CLOSED, INNOCENT), transformation tags (BEFORE, AFTER, DAY 1, SO SATISFYING, RATE IT 1–10), CTAs (FOLLOW FOR MORE, COMMENT YOUR VERDICT, WAIT FOR THE END), red circle and arrows, 8-bit sunglasses, 3-2-1 countdown | Drop on the timeline in CapCut, Premiere or DaVinci; pop in with a 110%→100% scale bounce |
| `emoji/` | 20 large emoji at 512px (😂 💀 😭 👀 🔥 😳 🤯 🫣 🤡 💯 ✨ 🎂 🐶 🐱 🏆 ⚠️ ✅ ❌ 🤔 🫠) | Reaction pops and emoji rain |
| `frames/` | Full 1080×1920 overlays: speed-lines zoom, dramatic vignette, camcorder REC frame, POV top-bar template, before/after split | Place over the whole video |
| `sfx/` | 13 synthesized WAVs: boom hit, whoosh, pop, ding, error buzzer, dramatic sting, stamp thud, cartoon boing, record scratch, riser, camera shutter, crickets, cash-register ding | Boom on the hook text, whoosh on cuts, riser before reveals, thud on stamps |

`preview.jpg` is a contact sheet of every visual asset.

## Why there are no "famous meme" images
Viral meme stills (movie or TV frames, stock photos such as the distracted-boyfriend photo, cartoon characters) are copyrighted. In a monetized Short they can trigger Content ID claims or strikes. Use these original formats instead, or license images from a stock site.
