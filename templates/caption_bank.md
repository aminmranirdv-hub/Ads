# Editorial Caption Bank (original)

Starters for the channel's own commentary. **Always adapt them to the actual
joke.** These go in the boxed `Editorial` style, never in quotation marks,
and are never attributed to the comedian.

## Hooks (first 0–3s): frame the situation without spoiling it
- POV: you tried explaining something to your parents
- When you realise your own argument makes no sense
- He really thought he was winning this conversation
- Every family has this one relative
- Nobody warned him about this part
- Wait for the last line…
- This escalated VERY quickly
- Confidence: 100%. Logic: 0%.

## Reactions (after the punchline)
- He did NOT see that coming
- The audience needed a minute
- Plot twist nobody asked for
- The silence says everything
- That's a family emergency now
- Respectfully… what?

## French
- POV : tu expliques un truc à tes parents
- Quand tu réalises que ton argument tient pas debout
- Il pensait vraiment avoir gagné
- Ça a dégénéré TRÈS vite
- Attends la fin…
- Personne n'était prêt

## Darija (Latin)
- POV: katchre7 chi 7aja l walidik
- Kola 3a2ila fiha had chi wa7ed
- Tsenna lekher…
- Hadchi tla3 bzzaf b zerba

## Arabic (MSA)
- انتظر النهاية…
- كل عائلة فيها هذا الشخص

## Darija (Arabic script)
- ما كانش متوقع هادشي

## Rules
- One caption on screen at a time, 2.5–3.5s each
- ≤ 8 words; one key word in capitals at most
- Never cover the face or the subtitle line
- If the caption *explains* the joke, delete it
